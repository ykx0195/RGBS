## 正文说明

已放在 RQ2 的 Fig.7 阈值分析之后、正常准入补充之前，明确使用原主集合。新表编号 VI，准入表顺延为 VII，相关引用通过标签自动更新。

> **Cross-encoder coverage.** We re-encode the frozen main BGE portfolios and the same deidentified Test requests with MiniLM-L6, GTE-small, and E5-small-v2, without target-specific search or reselection (Table VI). All embeddings are unit-normalized; E5 uses the query: prefix on both sides. Coverage varies substantially across target encoders at the fixed numerical threshold of 0.85. Because similarity-score distributions differ, these results characterize fixed-threshold coverage rather than a robustness ranking at comparable operating points. Task relationships and answer validity remain unevaluated for the three target encoders.
